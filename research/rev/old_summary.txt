Summary: This is the first-use-case design assembled from the sixteen delivered research points of the Superintendent specification, offering Simon a short list and a recommendation for each of the seven decisions the specification named. The recommended shape is one Starlette-on-uvicorn systemd unit co-located with the music apps, serving a vendored buildless Preact page that holds a single same-origin WebSocket carrying JSON envelopes, with each app carrying its own adapter module on a daemon thread that dials the service and speaks the same frame vocabulary; the sequencer's grid is content in `composition.data` behind a new `StepGrid` helper, written by absolute sets applied in loop order, confirmed by an `ack` at application and a per-cycle event carrying the built version and the realised cells, with the playhead extrapolated client-side from per-beat anchors. The panel is drawn as DOM cells with a transform-moved playhead overlay and canvas layers for redrawn content, on a Raspberry Pi 5 kiosk conditional on a frame-time check, with a gesture set in which every two-contact gesture has a one-contact form. Assembly found one coupling the points had not weighed against each other: the adapter's queue-and-drain rule costs the clock nothing but puts the `ack`, and therefore the cell's face, up to one beat behind the finger, which is the requirement the specification's decided table states plainly, so a short list for the thread-to-loop crossing is given here and must be answered together with the pending look. Subsequence needs no change to its clock, its rebuild or its scheduling: the package change is two new modules, four small engine edits and a dependency-floor fix, and four heavier items are named as needing a wave. Fifty-three questions and twenty-six hardware-gated checks are filed as Subroutine items and grouped here by what they block; the three this assembly raised rather than inherited are #2021, #2019 and #2020.
